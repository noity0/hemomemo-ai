Ilm aik roshni hai: parhne wala banda apna raasta khud dekh leta hai.
Akhlaq aik aadat hai: narm zuban, sach bolna, waada nibhana aur muskurahat.
Sach ki koi qeemat nahi hoti kyunke wo sab se bari izzat ki buniyaad hai.
Sabr ka matlab thokar nahi khana, balke achanak ghabaraye baghair chal pade ga.
Shukr dil ko khush karta hai: jo kuch hai us par Allah ka shukr ada karo.
Dua aik aisi talwar hai jo mushkil waqt ma sa rasman chalati hai.
Iman aik yakeen hai: Allah hai, Uske Rasul SAW sunnati zinda hai.
Namaz dil ko har din dhone ka behtareen tareeqa hai.
Tilawat se dil ki ghabrahat kam hoti hai aur sukoon barhta hai.
Maa baap ki dua sab duaon ke aage qeemti hai, unka ehtaram zindagi ka amanat hai.
Sila rehmi ik ik rishte ko dor dikha diya karti hai.
Jhoot aisay saach ki terrah hamesha ulajhta hai, aur sachai ain intezaar karti hai.
Gheebat dil ko kharab karti hai, mera dost ye baat band karo.
Umeed aik such aisi cheez hai jo dil ke andar chand si khushboo bana leti hai.
Mehnat maa say aik ehsaan hai, aur dil ki talab khatam kar di karti hai.
Waqt aik daulat hai: roz subah plan likhna timar ko aasan banata hai.
Sukoon dhundna mushkil hai magar zikr, shukr, aur dua dil ko halka kirata hai.
Tauba aik khula aastaa hai: gunah ke baad sachi? maafi dil ki safai hai.
Rahem ka matlab dil narm hai, doosron ke dukhaon par muraj ho bohat khoob.
Insaaf such aik bolna aur makrooh maiqri ko bhi aage lao malik hai.
Aman aik aisi cheshma ai (nuzoorat) hai jo zindegi ke phool khilata hai.
Dost koi bhi ho, sachai aur vefaa ka saath har bar lo jata hai.
Ilm aur amal ka aur aage chalna hi deen ki asli raah hai.
Dil pareshan ho to zikr aur dua karo, phir sungli raah milega.
Aakhirat asli ghar hai, duniya aik acha hotel ki tarah hai.
Allah behtar karega Inshallah: himmat aur dua kahan gayi yaad rakho.
Ilm ki roshni har darkwaze pe aaj alag rang leti hai.
Sukriya aur mehnat, majboot imarath ka do architecture hain.
