===== MATH: HISAB-O-KITAAB (Arithmetic + Business) =====
Sawal: Percentage (feesad) — percentage percent feesad hissa kitna percent
Formula: Part = (P / 100) * Total
Field: Math / Arithmetic
Vars: P=percent rate; Total=kul value; Part=hisso-value
Detail: Percent nikaalna: Part = (P/100) × Total. Misaal: 20% of 50 = (20/100) × 50 = 10.
Sawal: Percentage Change (tabdeeli ki feesad) — percentage change badlav izzafa kami
Formula: Change% = ((New - Old) / Old) * 100
Field: Math / Arithmetic
Vars: New=nayi value; Old=purani value
Detail: Purani se nayi value ki tabdeeli % me: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100. Positive = izzafa, negative = kami.
Sawal: Profit Percent (munafa) — profit munafa faida kaarobar
Formula: Profit% = ((SP - CP) / CP) * 100
Field: Math / Business
Vars: SP=sale price (farokht kimat); CP=cost price (khareed kimat)
Detail: Munafa % = ((SP − CP) ÷ CP) × 100. SP > CP hone par munafa hota hai.
Sawal: Loss Percent (nuqsaan) — loss nuqsaan ghaata kaarobar
Formula: Loss% = ((CP - SP) / CP) * 100
Field: Math / Business
Vars: CP=cost price; SP=sale price
Detail: Nuqsaan % = ((CP − SP) ÷ CP) × 100. SP < CP hone par nuqsaan hota hai.
Sawal: Sale Price with Profit — sale price munafa ke sath farokht
Formula: SP = CP * (1 + Profit% / 100)
Field: Math / Business
Vars: CP=cost price; Profit%=munafa rate; SP=sale price
Detail: Munafa jama kar ke sale price: SP = CP × (1 + Profit%/100).
Sawal: Discount (raft) — discount raft bachat bazarmol
Formula: SalePrice = MP * (1 - D / 100)
Field: Math / Business
Vars: MP=marked price (asal tag); D=discount %; SalePrice=sale kimat
Detail: Discount ke baad kimat: SalePrice = MP × (1 − D/100). Misaal: MP=1000, D=10% → 900.
Sawal: Simple Interest (sada sood) — simple interest sada sood byaaj qarz
Formula: SI = (P * R * T) / 100
Field: Math / Business
Vars: P=principal (asl raqam); R=rate % per year; T=time (saal); SI=simple interest
Detail: Sada sood: SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100. Har saal asl raqam par ek jaisa sood.
Sawal: Simple Interest Total Amount — total amount sada sood ke sath
Formula: A = P * (1 + R * T / 100)
Field: Math / Business
Vars: P=principal; R=rate %; T=time (saal); A=total raqam
Detail: Kul raqam: A = P + SI = P × (1 + RT/100).
Sawal: Compound Interest (murakkab sood) — compound interest murakkab sood byaaj
Formula: A = P * (1 + R / 100)^T
Field: Math / Business
Vars: P=principal; R=rate % per year; T=time (saal); A=total raqam
Detail: Murakkab sood: A = P(1 + R/100)^T. Har saal sood asl + pichhle sood par bhi lagta hai. CI = A − P.
Sawal: Compound Interest n times per year — compound interest n dafa saalana
Formula: A = P * (1 + R / (100 * n))^(n * T)
Field: Math / Business
Vars: P=principal; R=annual rate %; n=times per year (saalana martaba); T=years; A=total
Detail: Saal me n dafa murakkab: A = P(1 + R/(100n))^(nT). Mahana = n=12, ruba-saalana = n=2.
